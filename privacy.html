Virtual currency, purchases, and economy records

REWIND stores your Orb balance, plan, daily Orb grant date, accumulated playtime used for plan eligibility, starcode if enabled, gamepass ownership and purchase price, and badge awards. This information is used to show balances and entitlements, process Orb purchases, prevent duplicate gamepass purchases, apply plan rules, and investigate disputes or abuse. Gamepasses are bought with Orbs; the current service does not collect payment-card details or sell Orbs for money. The purchase prompt and database record are used to confirm the in-platform transaction.

Experience, friends, and safety records

REWIND stores friend requests and accepted friendship links to apply friend limits and friends-only experience visibility. It stores experience privacy settings and activity entries (such as edits, scripts saved, pass/badge creation, and awards) for up to 12 days in the experience activity view; related transactional records may remain for as long as needed to operate the account, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Reports contain reporter account, target identifier, reason, details, review status, and timestamps. Multiplayer prototype presence stores experience ID, player account ID, and last-seen time; inactive sessions are excluded from live counts after 90 seconds. Browser editor ServerScript text and experience data-store keys/values are stored with the experience. Desktop project files are local unless you choose to upload or publish their contents through REWIND.

4. Children, minimum age, and supervised experiences

REWIND accounts are limited to people aged 13 or older. Signup asks for a date of birth and rejects dates indicating an age under 13. The date is self-declared; this implementation does not independently verify identity or age.

Users aged 13 or 14 must request a link to an existing REWIND account belonging to someone aged 15 or older. That account holder must approve the request and attest that they are the child’s parent/legal guardian or are authorized by that person. Until approval, the child cannot see designated supervised experiences in the published-place feed. Creators can mark a place as requiring supervision.

A 15+ account is a platform rule, not proof that its holder is an adult or has legal parental authority. The account link does not replace any consent required by law, and REWIND currently does not check identity documents or independently verify the asserted family relationship. Users must provide truthful age information. A parent or guardian should help a child understand this notice and the Terms.

For France, a child who is at least 15 may consent independently to certain online-service processing that is based on consent. Below 15, consent for those consent-based processing purposes must be given jointly by the child and the person holding parental authority. Age 15 does not create general legal adulthood or automatically establish capacity to enter every contract. The applicable rules depend on the processing and jurisdiction.

5. When content is public

When you publish a place, its discovery information (such as its name, description, genre, creator username, and approved icon) is intended to be visible to other visitors. Uploaded catalog assets are held for moderation; an approved asset may be displayed through the configured storage bucket. Do not publish content unless you have the rights and permissions needed to make it public. The present API saves scene/script data to the database, but the public site does not currently provide a full runtime for published places.

6. Cookies and local browser storage

The current account system uses one essential cookie named rewind_session. It is HttpOnly, used for sign-in, and set to expire after 30 days. In production it is marked Secure and configured for cross-site requests needed by the separate static site and API. The current build does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. The browser may separately cache ordinary site files.

7. Service providers and recipients

Information is made available only to people and providers who need it for the purposes above:

  • Render: runs the REWIND API and may process operational request/error logs.
  • Neon: hosts the PostgreSQL database containing account, session, creator, and supervision records.

  • Authorities or other parties: only where a valid legal obligation, lawful request, or necessary protection of users and rights requires disclosure.

The operator must confirm provider names, data-processing terms, storage regions, and international-transfer safeguards in the actual hosting dashboards. REWIND does not sell personal information.

8. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Account records and saved content are kept while the account remains active and are deleted or de-identified after a verified deletion request, subject to records that must be retained by law or to resolve security/legal issues.
  • Session records expire after 30 days; logging out invalidates the current session.
  • Age and supervision records are retained while needed to enforce the age/supervision choice, then removed with the account or when the link is ended. A rejected request is not intended to be kept indefinitely.
  • Uploaded files may remain in the bucket until the associated asset/account is removed and storage cleanup completes. Public copies, caches, or material copied by other users may take longer to disappear.
  • Render, Neon, GitHub, and storage-provider backups/logs follow their own documented retention schedules.

This prototype does not yet expose a self-service delete-account control. To request access, correction, deletion, or removal of a supervision link, contact the privacy address above. The operator should publish a working address and set a practical response period before public launch.

The operator should maintain backups, access controls, patch dependencies, incident procedures, and provider agreements as the platform grows.
