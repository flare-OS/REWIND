ACCOUNT SAFETY

Family supervision

REWIND accounts are for people aged 13+. Accounts for ages 13–14 must link to a REWIND account owned by someone aged 15 or older before they can access supervised experiences.

Age is not the same as legal authority.

A 15+ account may approve a link only if its holder is the child’s parent/legal guardian or has that person’s authorization. This account link does not independently verify identity or replace any consent required by law.
